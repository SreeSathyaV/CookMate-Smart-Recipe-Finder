Chicken Biryani

★★★★★ 4.8 (125 ratings)

A delicious and aromatic chicken biryani made with fragrant basmati rice, tender chicken, fresh herbs and flavorful spices.

🥣 Preparation 15 min
🔥 Cooking 30 min
👥 Servings 4 People
🧺

Ingredients

  • ✓ Basmati Rice
    2 cups
  • ✓ Chicken
    500 g
  • ✓ Onion
    2 large
  • ✓ Tomato
    2 medium
  • ✓ Ginger Garlic Paste
    2 tbsp
  • ✓ Biryani Masala
    2 tbsp
  • ✓ Curd
    ½ cup
  • ✓ Mint & Coriander
    1 handful
  • ✓ Cooking Oil
    3 tbsp
  • ✓ Salt
    As needed
🍳

Required Equipment

🥘 Pressure Cooker
🍳 Frying Pan
🔪 Knife
🥣 Mixing Bowl
👩‍🍳

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Marinate the Chicken

Add chicken, curd, ginger garlic paste, biryani masala, salt and a little turmeric into a bowl. Mix everything well and marinate for at least 20 minutes.

2

Prepare the Rice

Wash the basmati rice thoroughly. Boil water with salt and whole spices. Add rice and cook until it is about 70% done. Drain and keep aside.

3

Cook the Chicken

Heat oil in a large pan. Add sliced onions and cook until golden brown. Add tomatoes and marinated chicken. Cook until the chicken is almost completely done.

4

Layer the Biryani

Spread the partially cooked rice over the chicken. Add mint, coriander and fried onions. Add a little saffron milk if available.

5

Dum Cook

Cover the pan tightly and cook on low flame for around 15 minutes. Turn off the flame and allow the biryani to rest for 5 minutes.

6

Serve

Gently mix the layers without breaking the rice. Serve hot with onion raita, boiled egg or your favorite side dish.

💡 Cooking Tips

  • Use good quality basmati rice for better texture.
  • Do not overcook the rice before layering.
  • Marinating the chicken improves the flavor and tenderness.
  • Always cook the final biryani on low flame.
  • Let the biryani rest for a few minutes before serving.
